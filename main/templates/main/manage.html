Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I find my card ID and password?

Both are printed on your card. The card ID is visible on the front; the password is separate — keep it safe, as it's needed for every update.

How long does it take for a change to apply?

Updates apply immediately. New scans of your card will use the new destination right away.

I lost my password. What do I do?

Contact support with your card ID and proof of purchase, and we'll help you regain access.

Can I set any website as my destination?

Any valid http:// or https:// link. Cards pointing to illegal or abusive destinations will be disabled.

Can someone else change my card without my password?

No. Only someone with the correct card ID and password can update its destination.
